Finance Observability

Finance Observability is a personal application for one household of two people. It runs on the owner's own computer. It reads the household's financial statements (bank, credit card, loan, investment, insurance and payslip documents), builds a ledger with a full audit trail, checks it against the statement balances, and shows descriptive reports about the household's financial position, such as net worth and how long savings would last.

The two household members view the reports on their phones. The phones receive only an encrypted summary that the owner's computer prepares.

The application is not offered to the public, has no accounts or sign-ups, and does not operate any server that receives user data.

Google account access

Each household member can connect their own Gmail account with read-only access. The application uses this access only to find statement emails from senders the owner has configured and to save their PDF attachments to the owner's computer. It does not read other email, and it never sends, changes or deletes email.
